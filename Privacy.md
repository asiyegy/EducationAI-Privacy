**Information Collection and Use**

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

*   Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
*   The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
*   The time spent on the Application
*   The operating system you use on your mobile device

If you choose to create an account, the Service Provider additionally collects:

*   Your email address (when signing in with Email, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In)
*   Your display name (provided by you or by your sign-in provider)
*   A unique user identifier assigned to your account

Creating an account is optional. Core features of the Application (browsing templates, generating worksheets, and browsing the community gallery) work without an account. An account is only required for community interactions such as publishing worksheets, liking, commenting, and following other users.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

**User-Generated Content and Community Features**

The Application includes a community gallery where signed-in users can share content. If you use these features, the following content is stored on the Service Provider's servers and is visible to other users of the Application:

*   Worksheets you choose to publish (including their title and description)
*   Comments you write

Likes and follow relationships are also stored and associated with your account. Content you publish can be removed by you at any time from within the Application. The Application provides tools to report inappropriate content and to block users; reported content is reviewed and removed if it violates our guidelines.
